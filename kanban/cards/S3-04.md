Decisions recorded in `api/zones.go`:

- The status body is the plan's literal `{"error":"zone polygon invalid"}`, with a
  sibling `detail` naming the zone and the reason — the literal is the contract the
  console and docs/api.md name, the detail is what an operator can fix.
- **An open path is closed implicitly** (3 vertices = a triangle), and a payload
  that repeats the first vertex is normalized by dropping the repeat: the same rule
  stored twice with two vertex counts is two installations that disagree about one
  polygon.
- **A polygon with no area is refused** — collinear and repeated-point polygons
  enclose nothing, so the rule never fires while the operator believes it is armed.
- Zone writes do **not** re-render go2rtc's config (the sidecar knows streams, not
  rules); a test asserts the config write count is unchanged by a zone write.
