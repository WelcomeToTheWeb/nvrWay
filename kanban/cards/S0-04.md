**A skeleton must not be able to fake a gate.** Each script prints what it will
do, names the card that fills it, and **exits 2**; `verify.sh` says it out
loud: “a gate that prints pass without observing it is worse than no gate”.

job greps for it: while any `scripts/*.sh` still carries it, `harness=false`
and the E2E job is skipped. The same probe gates each job on the files it needs
(`server/go.mod`, `detector/pyproject.toml` + a real file in `detector/tests/`,
`web/package.json`), so CI is visibly skipped, never falsely green, and
S9-09 replaces the probe with the real harness.

skeletons until S9-08. The `scripts/.gitkeep` and `.github/workflows/.gitkeep`
from S0-01 are deleted in this commit, as that card recorded.
